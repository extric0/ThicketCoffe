Our story

Started with one fern and a bad landlord.

Thicket opened because the founders kept meeting friends in cafés that felt like waiting rooms — beige, rushed, forgettable. So they leased a corner spot with the best window light in the block and filled it with the plants they couldn't stop buying anyway.

Every cup is pulled to order, every pastry comes from the oven two doors down, and every table gets left alone for as long as you need it.
